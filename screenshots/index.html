Screenshots

See it in the live demo: every screen below, clickable and filled with demo data, at moatazeldebsy.github.io/backstage-platform-template/demo/.

All shots are from live clusters — mostly a local Kind cluster brought up with ./scripts/setup.sh + ./scripts/bootstrap-ai.sh, plus a few from the AWS EKS path. Unlike the demo, nothing here is mocked.

Some tabs show live data from third-party integrations (SonarCloud, Datadog, PagerDuty, …) that will be empty on a fresh install until you add your own credentials — see Optional third-party integrations.

AI/ML platform and agents​

Ask it anythingIt plans the scaffold
