# Provision Team Namespace

Self-service team onboarding — creates a Kubernetes namespace with RBAC, resource quotas, network policies, and LimitRange.

## How to use

1. Open Backstage → **Create**
2. Find **Provision Team Namespace** and click **Choose**
3. Fill in the required parameters and click **Create**
